What we did, and why it works

AI doesn't pick a practice at random. It searches the web, checks what it finds, and names the practices it can describe and confirm. Everything we did since September feeds that check.

How ChatGPT decides who to recommend

ChatGPT's own words on EVO ICL: it recommends Commonwealth because the practice lists its EVO ICL surgeons for Louisville patients. That is the Louisville page we published on October 28.
A real Louisville office page

Published October 28 with the address, phone, surgeons and procedures in one place.

Why it helps AI needs one page that says who, where and what. ChatGPT now recommends you for EVO ICL because this page names the surgeons.

Doctor bio pages

Rewritten and approved for Dr. Piracha, Dr. Wörtz and Dr. Kuc, with details search engines can read.

Why it helps AI names surgeons, not just practices. Both surgeons now appear by name in the EVO ICL, PRK and lens exchange answers above.

Outdated posts retired

Old, thin posts were retired and redirected on October 15, with your approval.

Why it helps Google's core updates push down sites full of thin pages. Fewer weak pages and more in-depth ones is how a site recovers.
