Part 4 · Why the two offices move at different speeds

Lexington adds to a lead. Louisville is building one.

Both offices are better off than in September. The difference is where each one started, and where we put the work.

Lexington
Started near the top

In September, your site was already in Google's top 3 for 9 Lexington searches, and the main Google listing carries about 1,200 reviews. Each new page adds to that lead. Gains here show up as more searches and steadier rankings, not as a jump from nothing.

Louisville
Started from almost nothing

In September, ChatGPT did not name Commonwealth for any Louisville search we track, and the office had no real page of its own. Weekly Google posts, the Louisville page, doctor bios and directory listings were all aimed here, so every piece is the first of its kind. That is why Louisville has moved further in nine weeks.
