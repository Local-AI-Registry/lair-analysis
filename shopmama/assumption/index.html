How the forecast works
  • This forecast assumes the category launch lands: Dr. Rowen as the expert voice and the clinical studies and white paper in every ad and pitch.
  • Actual sales are Mama Sol's online sales from Shopify (Online Store, Shop app, Facebook and Instagram, subscriptions). Wholesale and events are not included.
  • November and December 2026 are estimates for the pause, based on the same months last year.
  • Sales without new spend follow 2026 by month, 25% lower because the old paid media is off. May's one-time spike is set to April's level.
  • The 2027 ad budget follows the season: $1,500 to $2,000 a month to test in winter, rising to $8,000 in June, then back down in fall. On top of that pattern, the budget grows about 2.5% a month as results prove out, so June 2028 is about 40% bigger than June 2027. Management adds 20%. SEO and PR stay at $2,000 and $2,500 a month.
  • Paid media splits into two parts. About $1,000 a month goes to Google search ads on the Mama Sol name and Lipbrella, which brought in about 26 orders a month on about $900 in July 2026, so we assume about $40 per order. The rest goes to Meta (Facebook and Instagram). Mama Sol's only Meta report, from August 2025, shows about $110 per purchase on the old positioning. We start there in January and assume the new Biological Suncare creative brings it down to about $45 by June. Bigger budgets cost a little more per customer, and 2028 costs about 15% less as winning ads are known. This is the number we check at 60 days.
  • Average order is $65 at today's prices, from leading with duos and sets ($60 to $162).
  • About 3.5% of past customers reorder each month, helped by subscriptions and refill emails.
  • SEO brings no sales until June 2027, since a new site takes months to rank, then keeps climbing through 2028.
  • PR lands three features a year, each bringing about 70 orders over two months, plus a few orders a month from smaller mentions. May 8, 2026 (50 orders in one day) is the guide.
  • Seasonality follows US Google searches for "mineral sunscreen", which peak in June.
  • Revenue is online sales on shopmamasol.com after discounts and returns, before tax and shipping.
  • Not included: QVC (about $250,000 in orders placed in 2026, with about $200,000 more expected for 2027, per Mama Sol), TV segments such as the Today Show (about $40,000) and a possible Good Morning America segment in spring, wholesale, Bloomingdale's and other retail partners. These sell outside the online store.

Sources: Shopify Analytics for Mama Sol (Nov 18, 2026); Google Ads search volume via DataForSEO; Triple Whale Meta ads benchmarks; shopmamasol.com prices. A planning model, not a promise.
